# What changed

One plain line per change a person would notice, newest version first. The update card and the update walk-through read this file (CHANGES.json holds the same lines). Add a new version's lines at the top when its number is raised.

## 0.1.19
- Looks: Studio, Midnight, Poster and Chartroom removed; new pages open in Glass dark
- Cards: each question on a card is answered and acted on separately; the card closes when all are answered
- Learning: a score for each kind of work, the coach proposes pick changes, levels 2 and 3 act on their own with Undo or a morning summary; only reply drafts can send, to the person who wrote

## 0.1.18
- Learns how you write, by email and texts and to whom, and drafts in your voice; your edits to its drafts teach it
- Roadmap: Questions for you pulses red and Still to check pulses amber while something waits

## 0.1.17
- I now speak to you as I, in your own words, across the page, cards, errors and history.
- Settings shows the version you have, beside its title.
- Asking me to handle one email by description ("my newest email") is done at once, on the newest one.
- Texts you send from the page now count as sent from the page.
- One reading of your mail now both judges it and writes its cards.
- Ask me how many cards, or which ones, and I search every card, open and closed.
- A card's choices now grow from what you did instead of my pick last time.
- Setup now runs on cards on your page and looks back over your history; Contacts come a page at a time.
- On the roadmap, each session's questions file shows as a read-only copy under Questions.

## 0.1.16
- Starship look: buttons keep their color when pointed at

## 0.1.15
- The new looks' names say where each came from

## 0.1.14
- Fifteen new page looks beside the current six, in Settings

## 0.1.13
- The mail-sending guardrail now refuses only real sends: a script written and run in one command is judged by what it does, and importing or naming the mail program is no longer a send.

## 0.1.12
- Every card now shows my pick first: what I think you'll do with it. One press does it.
- When you do something other than my pick, I note it, and the next card like it offers what you did ("Last time: ...").
- I now note what you do outside the page: mail and texts you send, and invitations you accept, decline or move in your calendar.
- New Drop button on every card, for a card you don't want considered. Done is for a task you finished; Got it shows only on cards that ask nothing.
- Each check's log line says what my picks cost.

## 0.1.11
- Roadmap: switching the roadmap off now saves its records first, in the project's .claude/roadmap-saved folder, and switching it on brings them back from there.
- Roadmap: Claude can now switch the roadmap off and on, and retire a project's old roadmap locks, when you say yes.
- The mail guardrail looks at what a command actually runs: a search of the mail program goes through, while a send is still refused.

## 0.1.10
- Roadmap: the lock now stops only real harm to the roadmap's own safety files, and judges a command by what it would write, not by the file names it mentions.
- The mail guardrail lets PowerShell commands that only read the sending program go through.
- Roadmap: it is now a log of the project; every decision and change goes on it.
- Roadmap: a session's questions file shows on the roadmap, tagged From chat; answer there or in the chat.
- Email: a Card button beside Archive, Delete and Junk, and inside an opened email, turns the mail you pick into cards at once, grouped by topic; an email already on a card opens that card.
- Email: when the mail service is busy, a press it took still reports done and the page catches up; a calendar change says it will show at the next check.

## 0.1.9
- The mail guardrail now refuses only a real send of mail; everything else in a terminal command is left alone.
- There is no To Delete anywhere now: what you delete is deleted. An update keeps safety copies of the older version in Live\Update copies, the newest two.
- Reading the program's own files is always allowed.
- A missing guardrails file now blocks only sends, nothing else.
- Roadmap: the lock covers only its own safety files, and git merges are allowed.
- "Tell the writer first" and the end-of-reply hold are now reminders, not blocks.

## 0.1.8
- Roadmap: the lock now protects only the roadmap's own safety files (the project's roadmap records, settings and hook scripts, and the plugin's five safeguard scripts); every other plugin file can be edited and committed from a project with a roadmap.

## 0.1.7
- Settings, Automation, Rule book: every press you make on a card that came from a message is kept there beside what the assistant did with it, by part of the assistant; Keep marks one reviewed, Edit says what the right answer was (with a note if you like) and Retire stops it counting.
- Settings, Automation, Learning levels: each part of the assistant (mailings, bills, what reaches the page, calendar, reply drafts, follow-ups) has its own level, 1 I suggest (the start), 2 It suggests, I approve, or 3 It improves itself, with how often its latest check matched what you did.
- At learning level 2, when your answer shows the assistant got something wrong, it puts one change of its own on a card (Rule change: ... or Setting change: ...), saying how many of your past corrections it fixes and breaks; Promote or Apply makes it, Reject drops it, and nothing changes until you press.
- Settings, Automation, Rule book: Start fresh moves your own rules and lists to To Delete after asking you, so the assistant learns them again from your cards; core rules, the rule book and your cards stay.
- Cards: a card from a mailing has Hide mailings like this, and a card from someone outside that needs nothing of you has Keep showing these; each is kept in the rule book and offers a rule card, and nothing changes until you press Approve.
- Cards: a button's label now wraps onto two lines in the same button instead of being cut short.
- The built-in safety checks are called guardrails everywhere you read about them; "rule book" now means only your own answer key in Settings.
- Cards: a message the assistant would have hidden, that none of your own rules or settings covers, now shows as a short "I'd hide this" card with Hide things like this and Keep showing, so you decide what it hides.
- Settings: a part of the assistant can be set to learning level 3, where it changes your rules there on its own, only when the change matches more of your past answers and breaks none; each such change is in Change history with Undo.
- A new assistant starts with nothing hidden: Filter strength starts on Show more, and setup no longer asks for junk senders or kinds of mail never to show; you teach it what to hide from your cards. An assistant set up before keeps its settings.
- Core rules (the goals and the built-in junk rules) are now part of the program, so updates reach them: they are no longer copied into your assistant's folder and cannot be changed from the page. Start fresh moves only your own rules.
- Rules: every rule now carries a line on why it is there, shown on the card that proposes it and in Settings, where you can change it; the assistant is told the reason with the rule.
- Talk to your assistant: put away now puts a card away whatever it still needs, and a mail search in an account covers its sent mail too.
- Cards: your answer to a card's question is acted on: a reply to whoever asked is drafted for your Send, and anything you ask for, like a calendar entry, is done.
- Calendar: your browser no longer fills in an event's Notes box by itself.
- Questions file: the line "Open questions for the person from this session, oldest first." is gone.
- Cards: Waiting is split into Waiting on others and Someday, each its own button with its count.
- A rule you approve on a card now reaches every open card it matches, including ones that only match by the email's subject.
- Talk to your assistant: sending several emails during a check no longer refuses some as "already sent"; if the page is busy it says so and nothing goes out.
- Talk to your assistant makes a change card only when you ask for a change or rule: a message that is only a remark or a question no longer gets one.
- Where the roadmap page cannot reach Claude, it now says why (no session watching, not seen as an editor, sending off in this view, claude.ai could not say, or the click not counted as yours) and writes the same reason into the comment it leaves for Claude.
- The check after each message in a roadmap project now also reads the page's settings requests, so a press in Settings is acted on at once.
- The manual is written again from the plugin as it stands: every part checked against the code, its pictures redrawn from your page's current look with made-up data (the change and rule cards, a card's own conversation, Open in the Claude app, the Email, Texts and Calendar parts, the Inbox and Tools settings, an attachment in the pop-up and the roadmap page's Settings), and nothing described that the page no longer has.
- Your page answers quickly while the 15-minute check is writing: reads that took over half a second now take under a tenth.
- Cards: a proposed rule, a standing approval offer and a project write now have their own No button in Done's place, and Done only marks a card done; every press is recorded with what it meant.
- Guardrails: Claude working in your assistant's folder is no longer stopped for a command that only reads, searches or names the file that sends mail; a command that would actually send is still refused.

## 0.1.6
- A roadmap's settings no longer fail when a closed Claude session left an empty or unreadable file behind: that session is skipped.
- Each card now has its own conversation: what you type on a card stays with that card and no longer shows in Talk to your assistant. One card is open at a time, and "this card" in the box means the open one (with none open, it asks which you mean); a card you name in words is found, and what you say about it up top is added to that card too.
- A change to how your assistant works, or a rule, asked in Talk to your assistant or the Claude app now becomes its own card with Approve, Disapprove, Discuss and Cancel; Approve on a change is its go, and a rule is saved only on Approve. The Change how your assistant works link and the banner under the box are gone. A rule you state on one of the assistant's own question cards is offered the same way, and a reply of two paragraphs no longer shows twice in a card's Conversation.
- Each roadmap page has its own Settings section for its project: the roadmap's kind, who writes it, each session's questions file and the model helpers use; a press there goes to the project's main session, and the page shows the new choice once it is made. Who writes the roadmap shows the command to run yourself, with Copy. Roadmap rows are named as the part of the work, a colon, then what is true once it is finished. Cards follow Sort cards by alone: an answered card is no longer put first in its group.
- Your assistant now knows a card's date is when it was made or last answered, not a due date: asking it to redate a card or bring it to the top just works, and when your words could mean two things it asks one short question.

## 0.1.5
- A PDF or picture attached to an email now opens in the pop-up window, with Download, Back to the email and Close; other files still download. An email sent only as formatted HTML now shows formatted when a card opens it.
- An update, or a check you start by hand, no longer cuts short a 15-minute check that begins while it is stopping the programs: that check is now skipped cleanly, and the next one runs as usual.
- Their newest email on a junk card, and links that leave the page (a sender's unsubscribe page, a full roadmap, the manual), now open in the same window the card's links use; if the browser blocks it, they open as before.
- Pressing one of a card's own answer buttons no longer leaves the card showing Reading... for many minutes: the press is always taken as a button press, wherever the card keeps its buttons.
- You can talk to your assistant from the Claude app: press Open in the Claude app under Talk to your assistant on your page (or type /assistant:talk in a Claude session opened in the assistant's folder). What you say there and what it answers show in the box on your page, marked In the Claude app, it sees what you said in the box, and it does things through the same checks as the box.
- When you answer a card, by typing or with one of its question's choice buttons, the card's date becomes the time you answered, so it sorts first under Newest first. Snooze, Done, Not needed and Approve do not move it.
- Texts: a person saved in your contacts under only a number is now shown by the name their own texts carry, the same in the conversation's heading and on each of their texts.
- A card no longer shows your assistant's reply in a box above its title: the reply is in the card's Conversation when you open it. Only a send that failed keeps its warning box there. The note after you press a button on a card now shows the words of your answer, never its raw record.
- Talk to your assistant can now organize your mail in every connected mailbox, Gmail and Outlook: move, label, star, mark read or unread, archive or delete one email or many, and make, rename, move or delete folders and labels. A folder that still holds mail is deleted only after it tells you what is in it and you say yes.
- It can also attach a file to an email (one from your mail, or one on your computer you name), set your email signature, mark texts read, take back a text before it goes or throw away a text draft, bring a hidden message onto your page, rebuild your cards, and mark, answer or write a note on a project's roadmap. A calendar card's Add to calendar now really adds the event.
- When a change to how your assistant works is being talked through, the banner under the box now says the Yes, make this change button appears once the change is agreed, and always offers Cancel this change.
- Talk to your assistant, and a reply typed on a card, can now do what the page's own buttons do: add, move, rename or remove a calendar event; send an email or a text when you tell it to send (it says exactly what went, to whom and from which address); press a card's buttons such as snooze, junk or drop; and refresh your mail. Nothing is sent or changed on a calendar unless your own words in that message ask for it.
- A Microsoft mailbox catches up faster: its one-time change to permanent email ids goes 200 at a time, keeping its place after each and never running past its time, stored emails are looked up together instead of one by one, and mail gets 60 seconds of each check instead of 40.
- A correction on a card, such as "I don't need to see these, this is on autopay", is now proposed as a rule under the box; and when the card's answer would itself offer to save a rule, the same Approve, Disapprove, Discuss and Cancel row holds it instead of a question in the reply.
- One background job instead of two: the job that shows your page now also reads your accounts every 15 minutes, and it no longer reports a refused start in Task Scheduler while it runs fine. An update removes the old separate reading job, keeping a copy of it in To Delete.
- On a card, Save now sits beside the box at all times and sends your words and closes the card in one press; the card stays open only when something still waits on you. Words that read as a standing rule are proposed as a rule under the box with Approve, Disapprove, Discuss and Cancel; an approved rule, from a card, I did not need this or Talk to your assistant, goes over your other open cards oldest first and says how many it changed, with one Undo.
- A Microsoft address's folders are now read before the rest of a check's work, so their refresh is no longer skipped when that work runs long, and the Email part picks up changed folders within about half a minute.
- The Mail part is now called Email. Its mailbox column lists every address you have, each with its folders (one whose folders are not stored yet shows its Inbox), and pressing an address's name only opens or closes its folders, leaving the list of emails as it was; the line down the left side and the line under a shut address are gone.
- A card's emails, papers and pictures open in a window of their own, the same window each time, so the page and the card stay put; on a phone they open in a new tab, and where the browser blocks the window they open over the page as before.
- Pictures texted during the days the rebuild redid stay stored and shown but are no longer queued to be described; a check describes at most 9 texted pictures, the newest first, and only with time left over after judging and writing cards.
- A busy day of messages is judged in rounds sized to finish in time (whole conversations, up to 40 messages or 40,000 characters each); a round that runs out of time is tried again at half the size, down to 5, and long attachment text is cut with a note saying how much was left out.
- Group by kind and Sort by now sit at the top of Cards, beside the filter buttons, instead of in Settings. Sort by offers Due date, Newest first, Oldest first, Waiting longest, Importance and Recently active; with Group by kind off, every card the filter shows is one list in that order. Due date now puts every card with a due date first, the soonest first.
- Every card shows the date and time it is from at its top right: when the first message behind it came in, or when the card was made if no message made it. Newest first and Oldest first in Sort cards by now go by that date, and under Card order the cards within each kind follow Sort cards by.
- While your old days are being rebuilt one by one, writing the cards runs on Sonnet; judging them keeps the model you chose, and normal checks are unchanged.
- Mail is as tall as Texts, and its list and the open email scroll inside it instead of stretching the page; folded, it shrinks to its heading as before.
- Folding a part while you are scrolled down inside it brings its heading back up, just under the top bar, so you do not lose your place.
- Dismiss is now Put away, and every message says "the next check" for the regular check of your accounts.
- Pictures in texts now come in at full size, not the small copy Google sends first: when only the small one is ready it shows at once and the full picture replaces it in that same text when your phone sends it. Pictures from the last week that came in small are replaced at the first catch-up after this update.
- An owner's own addition can bring a part of the page of its own, its own doors, entries in Tools, a hold rule for what reaches you, and its own kind of account.
- Settings, General, Calendar: the calendars shown on Today, ticked with their colours. "Choose calendars" on Today opens it.
- Pictures sent while your phone is still downloading them (several at once, say) now come in: the text shows first as a picture with no words and the picture fills into that same text once the phone has it, instead of staying a text with no picture. A text left that way in the last week is filled in at the next catch-up.
- Your words, as you chose them: messages kept off your page are Hidden (the filter button too), a card is snoozed with Snooze, the box at the top is Talk to your assistant, the approval level is "Do it and notify me", a phone message is a notification, and the tool is the Writing style builder.
- Roadmaps: a press stops saying "Sent, not yet on the roadmap" as soon as the roadmap holds it, even if Claude has since marked it done.
- Every card looks alike: a card put off with Later is a regular card under Waiting, each answered card under Done is a regular card with its own Undo, and held-back messages are regular cards tagged Held back. Errors says "Sync is off" once.
- One name for each thing on your page and in Settings: the scheduled run is "the next check", thinking runs are "AI runs", and the Settings parts, Held back and Ask first are called the same everywhere.
- Done is the one word for finished cards: the setting reads "Keep Done cards for", and Done cards sit in the list with no fold of their own.
- Sorting new messages is fast again and keeps up with a backlog: building what the judge reads took over three minutes on a large mailbox (one database opening per message) and now takes seconds; a backlog is judged a round at a time with time kept every pass for writing the cards of what was judged, so judged mail always becomes cards; a failed judge no longer holds back those cards for an hour; and an update no longer leaves Settings showing Sync Off, which a later save of Sources could write back and switch Sync off.
- Settings, Appearance: a small preview beside Theme, Style and Text size shows how your cards will look before you save.
- Settings: an (i) beside every setting and Tools entry opens a short note saying what it does, its default, and when a change takes effect.
- If your calendar can't be reached, the Calendar part tries again once a minute instead of over and over.
- Roadmaps: the whole roadmap now shows right in its own section of your page, where you mark checks, answer questions and write notes as before, instead of opening as a separate screen.
- Held back today is one card at the end of Cards, with how many and See them; the list itself shows under the Held back filter. A thinking step that only ran out of the pass's time is no longer shown under Errors: the pass log says it was put off, and the next pass goes on with it.
- The card sort choice "Important senders first" is now "Important first", which says what it does.
- A pass that runs out of time partway (often the first one after Sync was off a while) leaves the rest for the next pass, 15 minutes later: the messages it did not judge are judged then, not an hour later, and the page refresh at its end still gets time to finish.
- Saving a Writing style change (Tone, Greetings, Sign-offs, Common phrases, Phrases to avoid) now says it was saved. Before, the change was saved but the page said "Not saved. Something went wrong". Pressing an empty time or day in Calendar no longer opens the New event form; the New event button does.
- Your cards are one list, Cards, with no headings or folds inside it. Buttons across its top narrow it, each with its count: All, Needs you (where it opens), Waiting, FYI, Done and Held back, and each card carries a small tag with its button's name. Which kind comes first under All is yours in Settings, Appearance, Card order. Every card and button works as before, and an arrangement you saved maps onto Cards by itself. Ready to send is now Drafts, at the top of Mail: Mail's header says how many are ready to send, and its Drafts button opens them there (a new one opens them by itself). Changes to your assistant is now Change history, in Settings under Tools, each with Undo.
- Calendar: each calendar's coloured box hides or shows its events in every view (remembered on that device), and New event opens a short form that adds an event to one of your Google calendars through the sign-in invites already use; it shows at once, even while Sync is off.
- Settings speak plainly: the groups are General, Inbox, Automation, AI and usage, and Tools, and every part, label, choice and help line uses short app-style words (Gathering is now Sync, On or Off; How often it gathers is Check every; Your voice is Writing style). Messages on the page say Sync too ("Sync is off", "Sync is behind"), and the Tools entries are shorter.
- Texts: two copies of a text that differ only in spacing (a backup keeps two spaces where Google Messages keeps one) are one text: the clean-up of texts stored twice runs once more and keeps one, and a text coming in is matched to one already stored the same way.
- A Calendar part on your page (beside Texts, in the section menu): the events your assistant already gathers from your calendars, in Day, Week, Month and List views, one colour per calendar, in the page's own colours, light or dark, and at phone width. Pressing an event shows its time, place, calendar and description. Read only for now.
- Settings opens on five groups, by what you do (You and this page, What comes in, What it does for you, Thinking and cost, Tools); a group opens to its parts, and a line at the top (Settings > group > part) takes you back at any step. The part "Shown here, changed with a command" is gone: accounts, sending email, where your page lives, projects and roadmaps are Tools entries now, each with a button that shows the exact command to paste into Claude on your computer, and Copy. Every setting was checked against what reads it; none was found doing nothing, so none was removed.
- Gathering (Settings, What comes in, What it watches) is now the real switch: it reads Paused whenever the scheduled check on your computer is switched off, and Running or Paused switches that check on or off. A check run by hand still runs while the scheduled one is off.
- The texts link's log says, for every picture it sees (with its conversation and time), whether it had a media id, a thumbnail or inline bytes, and how the download went. The one-week catch-up for pictures runs again whenever the texts link's program is new, and every catch-up reads all the conversations with a text since it starts, not only the newest 25.
- Texts: a picture sent or received through the texts link comes in even when Google first reports it before the picture is ready, or offers only its preview or its bytes inline (as the bridge takes them): the text is handed over once the picture is there, or again with it, and the picture goes onto that text. Every message the link does not hand over is written in its log with the reason.
- Texts: pictures show in their conversation (press one to open it full size). A picture that came before the texts link, stored as a text with no words, is filled in from Google Messages: right after this update the link reads the last week again for that, and from then on each catch-up does; the texts log says how many were filled. A text stored twice before the link (same words, same conversation, same minute) is kept once, the copy written to To Delete first; the log gives the count.
- Texts: one person's threads (from a backup, the old extension and the texts link) show as one conversation: threads with the same Messages conversation or number are joined, and so are one-to-one threads of the same name whose numbers do not differ, even when Google Messages keeps two conversations for that person (a text and a chat one); it is answered in the newest, and its old links still open it. Google's list marks some participants as not visible (the bridge ignores them as fake); they are no longer taken for one of the people, which had made most one-to-one conversations look like groups and left them unmatched. When Google Messages keeps several conversations for one number (a text one and a chat one), the one with the newest text is matched, and a short code (a service's 5 or 6 digit number) is matched by its digits. The texts link reads Google Messages' whole conversation list, inbox and archive, page by page (each page with its own time limit), each time it connects; the texts log says how many it listed, how many older conversations were matched, and what the rest are.
- Texts: replying from a conversation stored before the texts link works again. The link reads Google Messages' conversation list when it connects and in each catch-up, and every older conversation it lists (one-to-one and group, including your notes to yourself) is matched to it by number, name or members; a one-to-one conversation it does not list is sent to the number its texts show. The texts log says how many were matched and how many were not, and a conversation that still cannot be answered says what to do instead.
- Texts: while texts are paused because Google Messages is open in your browser, a Take it back now button beside that line takes Messages back at once and catches up; the section shows when texts are connected again, or says so if Google kept it with the browser.
- After an update your page is always running again when its job is on: the update used to restart it only if it found it running, but it had stopped it itself a moment before looking, so the page (and the texts link) stayed down until someone started it.
- Texts: a setting for when Google Messages is opened in a browser (Settings, What it watches): Take it back right away (as before, the starting choice) or Let me use Messages in the browser; reconnect when I'm done, which pauses texts here (the Texts section says so) until the 15-minute check takes the session back and catches up. Google sends no word when the browser tab closes, so the check is what reconnects.
- Texts now come and go only through the texts link straight to Google Messages: the Chrome extension, its reader, its connection code and its send queue are gone, and the Texts section and the Errors box speak only of the link (connected, or disconnected with how to sign in again). Texts stored earlier stay. Right after this update the link reads in the last week of texts once (a text already stored is never stored twice), so texts missed while the extension was failing come in. A text goes only to a conversation the link knows or to a phone number.
- Texts: signing in to Google Messages, and the link's status, work on your page at its own private https address too (they were refused there, so the sign-in said "That did not go through"); they take the same checks as sending a text. If the sign-in cannot be reached at an address, the page says so plainly.
- Texts now come straight from Google Messages, with no browser tab: a small program that runs with your page (hidden, started and stopped with it) connects to Google Messages the way Messages for web does, hands every text to your assistant, and sends the texts you press Send on. Sign in once in the Texts section (Sign in to Google Messages): the steps show how to copy the sign-in from a Firefox private window, then you tap the emoji the page shows in Google Messages on your phone. When Google signs it out or your phone is unpaired, the Texts section and the Errors box say so plainly. Google lets only one web session be active, so once you have signed in, a Messages for web tab in a browser (the Chrome one the extension reads too) is taken back within seconds; the Chrome extension stays until texts are seen arriving the new way.
- Texts fixes, twenty-first round (extension 0.2.19): after an update the old reader kept running in the open Messages tab and the new one stood down, so the update changed nothing there. A reader now stands down only for a reader of the same version; a new version takes over, and every reader checks on each tick that it is still the one in charge and stops itself if not. The reader line in the texts log now also gives the reader's version and, for the top five conversations in the list, the time label shown and whether the conversation is due to be read (no names or words).
- Six new styles for the page replace the old ones (Settings, How the page looks, Style): Studio (clean, one strong blue; the starting style), Midnight (soft layered greys and violet, made for evenings), Poster (thick black outlines, hard shadows, bright yellow), Workshop (tall condensed headings, safety orange, a hazard stripe on errors), Chart room (navy and brass, serif headings) and Broadsheet (a newspaper: black rules, big serif headlines). Each comes in light and dark, cards stand clearly off the page, and every word and control meets the readability standard for contrast. A style saved earlier (Classic, Calm, Ledger, Hearth, High contrast, Terminal) shows as Studio until you pick again.
- Texts fixes, twentieth round (extension 0.2.18): texts of today were given an earlier day. The page writes a day divider as a date or a weekday for earlier days ("Thursday · 3:44 PM") and only the time for today ("7:43 AM", no "Today"), and the reader gave today's texts the day of the divider above. A divider with only a time is now today's. A text already stored with the day of the divider above it is given its right day when it is read again (same Messages id, same words, the same time of day, an earlier day). The reader line in the texts log now also shows the last four divider labels (dates and times only) and the newest text's time in the open conversation.
- Texts fixes, nineteenth round (extension 0.2.17): the reader now sends counts of what it saw and did with every report (conversations in the list, how many were due to be read, visited, not reachable, not matching the thread on screen, whether it counted as idle, messages found, already sent, new), and the assistant writes them to the texts log as a 'reader:' line whenever they change (and at least every ten minutes), so it can be seen why nothing is arriving. A likely cause is also changed: a visited conversation whose header the list writes differently (a saved contact) never counted as the right thread, so nothing was stored from it.
