# Texts from Google Messages (including RCS chats)

For Claude: the owner does this on his own page; you only tell him where.

How it works, to say first: "Your assistant links to Google Messages itself, the way Messages for web does, and reads and sends your texts through it, RCS chats included. It sends a text only when you press Send on your page."

1. On your page, open the Texts section and press **Sign in to Google Messages**. Follow the steps it shows: they end with tapping the matching emoji in Google Messages on your phone.
2. Texts then show in the Texts section; the last week is read in at once. If texts ever stop arriving, the Texts section and the Errors box say so and how to sign in again.
