# Junk rules

Core rules, part of the program: read by the judge, when sorting messages, beside the owner's own Junk rules. Updates change them; they are never copied into a home or changed from the page. The sender lists are data (Junk senders).

## Rules

- [559] (G9) An email with an unsubscribe link is junk before the judge reads it, unless its subject is about security, a sign-in, a password or fraud, or holds one of the owner's own keep words (the list in Settings on their page).
- [560] (G9) A text from a short number (6 digits or fewer) is junk before the judge reads it.
- [561] (G9) An email sender the judge calls junk three times in a row is raised once, and the owner's answer puts it on the Always junk list or the Never junk list.
- [562] (G9) The Never junk list, the owner's contacts, anyone they have written to, and their own domains override every junk rule, except that a sender the owner put on the Always junk list with the Junk button is junk even when a contact.
- [631] (G9) An Always junk sender is settled before the judge reads it; a Probably junk sender is still read by the judge.
