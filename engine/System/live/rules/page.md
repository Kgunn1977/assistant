The page, from top to bottom, as it is now. Parts of the page can be renamed for one owner's setup, so the page may show other words in those places; what each place holds stays the same. The owner's manual is on the page itself: the link "How your page works" is at the top of Settings and in the footer.

THE BOX AT THE TOP
- "Ask or tell your assistant anything", with a Send button. Enter sends; Shift and Enter starts a new line. One message goes at a time: Send waits until the reply is back. The conversation shows in the box; older talk folds under Earlier talk, and Clear the talk hides what was said so far on this device (nothing is deleted, and Undo brings it back). A reply that rests on their data has a Source line under it. Typed words go to Claude at once. Words typed on a card join this same conversation, marked with the card. If their computer is not listening, the words are kept and answered when it is back. The box can do what the page's own buttons do, by their words: add, change or remove calendar events, write emails and texts and send them when told to, press a card's buttons, clean up mail, refresh mail, keep tasks; each reply says exactly what was done. The box cannot change a setting.

JUST UNDER THE BOX
- "A newer version of this page is ready", with Load it now. It also loads by itself as soon as they are not typing. Shown only when there is one.
- Settings (or Close settings): always there. It opens the Settings screen, described below.
- Write an email (or Continue your email): only when sending from the page is switched on. When sending is off, a line says how to turn it on: type /assistant:change sending in Claude on their computer.

THE LINE AT THE TOP
- Under the page title and today's date is one plain line saying what is left. With work for the owner it reads "3 things need you.", then, when any are late or due, "1 is due today or late.", then "Next: " and the first card under Needs you, with its due day when it has one. With nothing left it reads "Nothing needs you.", and "2 things wait on others." when some do. When standing approvals did something today at the level "do it and notify me", it ends with "1 thing was done for you today."
- "Show it" (only when something needs the owner) goes to that first card: it opens the folds around it and flashes it. The line follows each press at once.
- A card that is new since the owner last looked on this device carries a New tag; that is kept on each device separately.

SEARCH
- "Search everything on this page" shows only cards whose words match. The list beside it (Everything) shows one group or one area. Show everything clears both. "Showing X of Y" says how much is hidden.

ABOUT YOUR ASSISTANT
- Shown only when there is something to read; its heading says how many, and it starts folded. Each line is one thing: "Sync is off" (nothing new comes in until they turn Sync back on in Settings, under Sources; answers on cards still work), "Sync is behind", "Today's limit on AI runs is reached" (cards wait; a typed answer is saved and read first the next day), "Your computer is not listening" (what they press is saved and waits until it is back), and any other problem the assistant knows of.

CARDS
- The cards are one part of the page, Cards (in the left column unless moved): ONE list, with no headings or folds inside it. Filter buttons across its top narrow it, each with its count: All, Needs you (the default), Waiting on others, Someday, FYI, Done, Hidden. These are the only names for the kinds of card; each card carries a small tag with its filter's name (Needs you, Waiting on others, Someday, FYI, Done). Beside the filters, Group by kind (On by default) and Sort by (Due date, the default; Newest first; Oldest first; Waiting longest; Importance; Recently active), each with an (i). With Group by kind on, the kinds stay together, in the order Settings, Appearance, Card order gives, and Sort by orders the cards within each kind; off, every card the filter shows is one list ordered by Sort by alone. Either way Hidden is last, and neither changes which cards a filter shows. A change shows at once and is saved for every device. The last card is always "Hidden today: 43." with See them, which shows the Hidden filter.
- Drafts: the emails written for them wait in Drafts, at the top of the Email part. Email's header says how many are ready to send, and its Drafts button opens them there (a new draft opens them by itself).
- Change history: every change made to how their assistant works, each with its Undo, is in Settings, under Tools, Change history.

FOLDING
- Every part of the page folds and opens when its heading is pressed (the groups in Settings too; those start shut): Errors, Cards, Projects and roadmaps, Today, Email, Texts, Calendar, and Earlier days (under the Hidden filter). Nothing inside Cards folds. A folded part still shows its heading and how many cards it holds. What is folded is remembered on each device separately. Everything starts open except Errors.

THE KINDS OF CARD (all in the one Cards list, each tagged with its filter's name)
- Needs you: the cards that need the owner (one press, their words, only they can do it, just arrived), in the order Sort by gives; by default the soonest due date first (late ones lead), then important, then the oldest. The card named after "Next:" in the top line is always the first of them. A card that is only theirs to do carries a small tag "Yours to do"; one that needs their typed words carries "Needs your words". Cards that came back sit first. Quick questions, often about one line of something, show "Best guess" instead of the recommendation and come after the others. A card they answered that now waits on someone else is not here.
- Waiting (longest open first): things the assistant or someone else is working on. A card says "Nothing is needed from you right now" and who it is waiting on. The assistant brings each back up on its check back date. Cards snoozed to Someday (Snooze, Someday) are here too, after them, each with Bring it back and Done.
- FYI: cards that need nothing from the owner, one Got it each; under the FYI filter, Done, all at the top closes every one shown.
- Done: what the owner pressed or answered, from the press until it is settled, each with Undo; cards the assistant marked Done with nothing done (a card joined into another, saying which and whether that one is open or how it ended; a card marked Done on the assistant's own reading, with its reason or "no reason given"; never shown as handled); cards finished in the last 7 days (or the 3, 14 or 30 chosen in Settings), each with Reopen, and under More Put away, which takes it off the page at once; and each use of a standing approval (see STANDING APPROVALS).

OTHER PARTS
- Projects and roadmaps: shown only when there are some. One fold for each project the owner introduced to their assistant. Notes a project sends in arrive as ordinary cards in that project's name. For a project with a roadmap, the roadmap itself is drawn in place inside its section and looks and works as the roadmap's own page does: marks (Works, Doesn't work, a comment), answers to its questions, notes on a row. What they press is sent at once to that project's own Claude session; until the roadmap's next copy shows it, the page says "N presses sent, not yet on the roadmap" and then clears the line by itself. If it could not be sent, the page says so in plain words, takes the change back and keeps what was typed. A roadmap whose whole copy has not reached the page shows a short summary and says so.
- Today (the side column): the calendar for today and the coming days, each event with its time (or All day) and place.

HELD BACK
- The last card in Cards is one card: "Hidden today: 43." with See them, or "Nothing was hidden today." See them shows the Hidden filter, which lists today's messages that came in and were kept off the page, newest first. Each row shows who and what, when, and "Hidden because: ..." with the plain reason (for example a mailing with an unsubscribe link, or one of the owner's own lists or rules).
- Each row has I should have seen this. Pressed, the message becomes a card within seconds, with no AI run, and the row says "It is on your page now." On a failure the row says so and the button stays.
- Each row also has a fold, Read it: From, To, When, Subject, the names of any attached papers, and its own words; a long message comes in parts, with Show the rest. Reading changes nothing.
- Earlier days (with a count) is a fold holding the days before, each with its own rows.

THE FOOTER
- One quiet line: "All is well" with when accounts were last read, or a pointer to Errors. A link, How your page works, opens the manual. Sign out is at the owner's own site when there is one.

A CARD, closed
- Tags: Returned (it came back after a press, with the reason), New, Important (an important item also sent one phone notification, when notifications are on), "Your yes is recorded, not sent", Due today, Due on a day or Was due. Days are written as Today, Tomorrow, Yesterday or a date, and the page updates them as the days pass.
- A short header (never cut off), then the one thing wanted from the owner, then a "Here because:" line saying why it is on the page. Pressing the header opens the card. A card that needs nothing has only Got it.

A CARD, opened
- Folds, when they exist: Everything it asks, What the assistant recommends (or The best guess), The story, The facts, The draft, a table of lines, Source message, Attached papers, Evidence, and History (everything the assistant has done on it, one line each). A last line gives its tags, area, status and age.

THE BUTTONS ON A CARD, and what each does
- Got it: on a card that needs nothing; marks it Done.
- Approve: yes to the recommendation or the draft as written. On a draft it records the yes and opens the writing screen; nothing is sent until the owner presses Send now.
- Change: opens a box, "What should change"; Save sends it, Cancel closes it.
- Answer buttons with set words: one or two show as buttons; more than two show as Choose.
- Answer (and Your answer, under More): opens a box in their words; Save or Cancel. The words go to this same assistant, Talk to your assistant at the top, with the card attached, and the talk shows in Talk to your assistant marked with the card. Words that change a fact the card states rewrite the card from the new facts; their words stay in History. An email written for the card goes to Drafts, in the Email part, not onto the card.
- On a calendar card: Add as shown adds the event at the day and time shown to their main calendar; Change opens Day, All day, Starts and Ends; Add to calendar adds that pick. The card then says what was added and on which calendar, or why it could not be.
- Edit (when sending is on and the card has a draft) and Reply by email (under More, on a card from an email) open the writing screen.
- Snooze: Later today (back in 3 hours), Tomorrow, Next week, Someday, or a date. Only the choices kept under Snooze options show. A card snoozed comes back by itself.
- Done: marks it finished; on a task it is the main button.
- More: Junk (a card from an outside sender) puts that sender on the owner's junk list at once and marks the card Done. Never junk makes sure their mail is always read. Undo takes either back. On a new sender's card the choices are Show their mail or Hold it.
- I did not need this (under More): Only this one, This sender (puts the sender on the list of senders to always ignore) and This kind of message (one AI run proposes a rule, arriving as a rule card; nothing changes until Approve). A card made by I should have seen this shows Always show these instead.
- An "I'd hide this" card: a message the assistant judged as needing nothing that neither the owner's own rules nor their Settings choices cover. Hide things like this closes it and offers a hide rule as a rule card (nothing changes until Approve); Keep showing closes it and offers nothing. Mail their own rules or Settings choices hide makes no such card. The setting that hides cards needing nothing never hides it, and I should have seen this on that message under Hidden brings this same card back rather than making another.
- At learning level 3 (Settings, Automation, Learning levels), the assistant changes the owner's rules in that part on its own, only when the change matches more of their past answers and breaks none; each such change is in Settings, Tools, Change history, with Undo. A setting change still waits for Apply.
- A rule card ("New rule: ..."): Approve saves the rule, applying from the next check, and goes over the other open cards as below; No drops it; Change (under More) rewrites the sentence, still not in force.
- The box on every open card, "Ask or tell the assistant about this card", has Save beside it at all times. Save (or Enter) sends the words and takes the card out of the way in one press; the card comes back open, with the answer showing, only when something still waits on the owner. Words that read as a standing rule ("from now on ...", "always ...", "handle all of these like this", or a correction or statement of how cards of that kind are handled, such as "I don't need to see these, this is on autopay") are not acted on: under the box the answer proposes the rule in one sentence, with four buttons. Approve saves the rule, acts on the card, then goes over the other open cards, oldest first, changing only the ones the rule covers (Done, put away, snoozed, retagged or rewritten; nothing is sent), and the card says "Your rule was applied to N other cards" with one Undo that puts them all back. Disapprove saves no rule and acts on the words for this card only. Discuss opens a box under the proposal to reword it; each new wording has the same four buttons. Cancel does nothing and puts the words back in the box. When the card's answer itself would offer to save a rule, it never asks in words: the same row with the four buttons holds that rule, and as the words were already acted on, Approve only saves the rule and goes over the other cards, and Disapprove only drops it. A rule saved in Talk to your assistant goes over the open cards the same way, with its Undo on the reply.
- A waiting card that came back: it returns after the set number of working days with no reply (3 unless changed in Settings) or as soon as that person writes again. Wait longer, Write to them, and Drop it (under More).
- The old-cards card ("Keep, snooze or mark Done each one"): appears when cards have sat untouched for the set number of days (14 unless changed). Each row: Keep, Snooze, Done; Undo for 15 seconds.
- A card from a project's note, when that project lets answers go back: typing an answer makes one more card that shows the exact file and words and asks first ("Yes, write it" or "No, do not"). Nothing is written into the project unless they say yes there.
- Undo: after a press a note shows at the bottom for 15 seconds with Undo, and the row in Answered by you keeps an Undo. A sent email cannot be undone.

STANDING APPROVALS
- A standing approval lets the assistant do one kind of thing in one area without asking each time. Nothing is done unless the owner made one, and every one starts at Ask first. Kinds: closing cards that need nothing, dropping waiting cards that came back with no reply, and sending replies to one person.
- The offer: after the owner has done the same kind of thing by hand several times (5 unless set to 3 or 10, or never), a card tagged Standing approval asks. Its answers: Yes, do it and notify me; Yes, do it quietly; No. A No is remembered and the offer comes back only after that many more. Only that answer ever makes one. Settings can pause one, change its level or remove it, never make one.
- Sending replies to one person is offered after they have sent several short replies to the same person by hand. The card names the person and the account, lists real examples of what it would have covered, and says the limits. Only replies to that person's own message are sent: one recipient, no copy, no attachment, no link, short. At most a few a day (Settings, Approvals, Daily send limit; 3 unless changed). Anything that does not clearly fit is not sent and comes to them as usual. Every send is listed with what was sent, to whom, and which approval allowed it. A sent email cannot be taken back. Pausing or removing it stops the next send at once.
- Approvals (Settings, under Automation): each entry names the kind and the area (and the person, for sending), a level (Ask first, Do it and notify me, Do it quietly), On or Paused, and Remove (pressed twice), with when it was made and how many times it was used. Add makes only the closing and dropping kinds. Nothing takes effect until Save.
- It acts at the next check. Done and notified counts in the top line; done quietly does not. Both are listed.
- The uses of standing approvals (in Cards, under Done): every use, newest first, ten shown and the rest under Earlier uses. Each row shows the card, when, the kind and area, and "done and notified" or "done quietly", with Undo for what can be undone. A sent reply shows what was sent and has no Undo.

THE WEEKLY REVIEW
- One card tagged Weekly review, made once a week: Friday at 3:00 PM unless the owner chose another day and time (Settings, Weekly review), or off. It is made from their own cards, what is snoozed to Someday and the calendar; it uses no AI run, and the card says so.
- It has a line of counts and three parts. Get clear: Waiting on you, Waiting on others past their date, Cards that have sat. Get current: Next week, What was hidden this week, What it learned. Get creative: Someday, with a plain prompt to pick one thing to start.
- Each part folds with its count. A row is one of their cards with the same buttons as elsewhere (Done, Snooze, Bring it back, Wait longer, Drop it, Keep). Done on the card marks the whole review Done.

THE SETTINGS SCREEN (opened by the Settings button)
It covers the whole page. Back to your page and the link "How your page works" are at its top. Groups fold until their heading is pressed; each has its own Save, and nothing is saved until Save is pressed. Under the button the page says what was saved and when it takes effect, or why not. "Put back what was saved" drops what was typed. The assistant in the box cannot change a setting; only the owner can, here.
- Profile: first name, full name, the sentence about what they do and who matters, the page's heading and the line under it, and their areas.
- Filters: filter strength; Rules (their own plain sentences, each Show less or Always show, each editable, On or Paused, with Remove and an Add a rule box; two rules that pull against each other are saved with a warning); priority senders; mail needing no action; first-time senders; newsletters and promotions; mailings kept by one of their words; automated notices; mail they are only copied on; texts from numbers not in their contacts; the "Here because" line; when a waiting card comes back; when it asks about a card that has sat.
- Sources: the important topics list, their own mail domains, senders to always ignore, words that keep a mailing in front of them, Sync (On or Off), how often it checks (15, 30 or 60 minutes).
- Notifications: phone notifications on or off, and quiet hours.
- Appearance: theme, text size, which sections show and their order, Card order, Group cards by, how long Done cards stay, the default snooze date, 12 or 24 hour times, Snooze options.
- Approvals: the standing approvals and the daily limit on sends under them.
- Weekly review: the day and time, or off.
- Sign-in: change the page's password, how long a sign-in lasts, Sign out everywhere (pressed twice).
- AI and usage: the AI model, the quick pre-sort, and a daily limit on runs.
- Shown here, changed with a command: which roadmaps show, the accounts connected and whether each works, projects introduced, sending email on or off, and where the page lives. None can be changed on the page. Each line says which command changes it, typed in Claude on their computer: /assistant:change (accounts, page, sending, read-back), or /assistant:introduce for projects and their roadmaps.

THE WRITING SCREEN (Edit, Reply by email, Write an email)
- From (the account it goes out from), To and Copy to (names they have written to are suggested), Subject, Message, and Attach (only files the card lists). A line says how many characters are left; one email holds about 3,500.
- Check and send shows exactly what will go (type, From, To, Copy to, Blind copy, attachments, the folded original); Send now sends it; Back to editing returns; Close keeps the words; Throw it away drops them. Nothing goes out unless they press Send now, tell their assistant to send it (in the box at the top or on a card), or a standing approval to send replies that they made allows it. The writer's Type switches between new, reply, reply to all and forward; From lists every sending account; attachments come from the original, another email or their computer, each removable; a send with a file that cannot be fetched is refused whole.
- Email (a part of the page, at the bottom unless moved): one inbox across every connected address, filter by address, Inbox, Unread or All mail, search (and Also search the mail services), a conversation with its files and labels. On each email: Reply, Reply all, Forward (a draft in Drafts, at the top of Email), Archive, Delete (to the trash, never for good), Label, Mark read or unread, each on their press. A mailbox signed in before 0.1.4 must be signed in again in Setup before Archive, Delete, Label or Mark read work; until then the page says so. If their computer is not listening, Send now waits and the words are kept.

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER A PRESS
- After a press or a typed answer a line at the bottom says what happened, with a link back (Show it, or Show the card).
- A plain press is acted on within a second with no AI run; typed words go to Claude at once. Every press is recorded.
- The card leaves its place at once and shows under Answered by you: Saved, then Your computer is working on it, then the result. When their computer is not listening, it waits until it is back.
- If the assistant could not act, the card comes back marked Returned, with the reason.
- If a press did not save, the card says so and they press again. Unsent words and open boxes are kept on that device through a reload.
- The page updates itself as new data arrives, without disturbing a card they are typing in.

WHERE THE PAGE CAN BE OPENED
- On the owner's own computer, at an address starting http://127.0.0.1: (only in a browser on that computer).
- From anywhere (a phone, another computer), when they switched that on: at an address starting https:// that a free program called Tailscale gives their computer. The same page, the same password. It does not open while their computer is off or asleep. It is switched on, checked and off only with /assistant:change page, typed in Claude on their computer; nothing on the page changes it.
The addresses in use are in the Settings screen, last group, under "Where this page lives".
